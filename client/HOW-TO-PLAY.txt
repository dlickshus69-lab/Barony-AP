Barony Archipelago

1. Copy everything in this zip into your Barony folder
   (Steam: right-click Barony > Manage > Browse local files), replacing barony.exe.
   Steam > Properties > Installed Files > Verify puts the original game back any time.

2. Start Barony from Steam as usual. The Barony Archipelago window opens first.
   Type your room's port (the digits after archipelago.gg:) and your name from your YAML,
   then press Connect & Play. The game starts and connects; the window stays open as your tracker.
   It remembers what you typed, so next time you usually only change the port.
   "Play without Archipelago" starts the normal game instead. Closing the window quits.
   (No password box: rooms without a password don't need one.)

The tracker window:
  Left       a map of the floor, filled in as you explore (like Barony's own map).
             Archipelago items (green) and green enemies show from the start; other items show
             once you've seen them, with their own icons. Hover a pile to see what's in it.
             The Floor Key shows only while the Compass or the key warning points at it.
  Right      connection, playtime (only time in the dungeon counts; it shows how long the
             seed took once you've won), floor, Floor Key, Minotaur, secret area entrance, counts, deaths, and tabs:
             Item log, Full log and Spells (Tab or click to switch). The mouse wheel over this
             column scrolls it; the map stays where it is.
  Map view: mouse wheel zooms, drag moves it, F = Follow (keeps you in the middle),
             R = Fit (whole floor), + / - zoom, arrow keys move. Buttons for these sit on the map.
  Make the window narrow to hide the map.
  Room link: paste your room's link (archipelago.gg/room/...) in the Server box instead of a
             port. The tracker finds the port itself, and if the room sleeps and comes back on a
             new port, it reconnects there by itself.

In game:
  Top left   Archipelago status, and how many Archipelago things are left on this floor
  Top right  lives, and the skull with the total death count
  F1  items you received      F2  full log      F3  deaths per player
  F4  tracker: full or short list of the normal loot on the floor (also L or a click in the tracker)
  BaronyAPTracker.exe opens by itself when you connect: a window with the same information
  (close it any time; it opens again the next time you start Barony).

Chat commands (type in the in-game chat):
  /ap_status    connection, lives, kills, waiting traps
  /ap_connect host:port slot [password]   connect by hand
  /ap_say text  chat with the room

DLC: start Barony from Steam and the DLC packs you own are turned on.
This build has no Steam online lobbies or achievements. Single player works normally.
